Better applications

Proposal 1

What we saw on the site today

We checked the public careers pages on 23 September 2026. There were 20 open roles across Sarajevo and Belgrade, in eleven languages.

  • The form asks for five thingsFull name, email, phone, a bio and a CV file. Language, level, city and start date are only in the CV, if at all, so they have to be read and typed in by hand.
  • Each job ad is one imageA "WE ARE HIRING!" poster with the duties, requirements and benefits written inside the picture, and an empty image description.
  • Google gets an image tagThe site already sends job details to Google in the right format, but the description it sends is the image, not the text of the ad.
  • Small typosThe job filter says "norvegian language", categories switch between "English Language" and "Italian language", and the footer has "Colarado" and "Metro Manilla".

The new application form

The same form candidates know, with four additions. Each one is something you currently dig out of CVs by hand, and each becomes a filter in the candidate list.

Apply for this position

Why these four

  • Languages and levelEvery role here is defined by a language. The level uses the European scale (A1 to C2) that your ads already mention, like "minimum B2".
  • Preferred cityMost roles are open in both Sarajevo and Belgrade.
  • Can start fromTells you who is available now and who is still in another job.
  • Keep on fileA clear, separate yes that lets you contact people about future roles. The length (12 months here) is your decision; the wording should be checked by your legal or HR team.

Candidates declare their own level, so it is a starting point for your language test, not a replacement for it.

Job ads as real text

The poster stays: it looks good and people share it. Underneath it, the same content becomes normal text on the page. We convert the current ads and set up a simple template, so a new ad is a matter of filling in a few boxes.

German language: Customer Support Agent

SarajevoBelgradeGerman, minimum B2Customer Support Agent
What you will do

  • Build rapport and aim to resolve each enquiry on first contact
  • Keep customer records up to date in line with data protection rules
  • Work with back-office teams and the client to reach a good outcome
What you bring
  • Excellent written and spoken German, minimum B2
  • Clear, friendly phone manner and good listening
  • Comfortable with computers and working to targets
  • Teamwork in a fast-paced environment

Text taken from the current German Customer Support Agent poster on the DDC site, shortened for the mockup.

What changes

  • Screen readers and browser translation can read the ad.
  • Candidates on phones can read it without zooming into a picture.
  • The site search and Google see the actual duties and requirements.
  • The job data sent to Google carries the real description, so the ads meet the requirements for Google's job listings.

What it does not do

  • It does not guarantee that Google shows the ads, or where. Google decides that.
  • It does not guarantee more applicants. It removes an obstacle; it does not create demand.

An automatic reply

A short, friendly email right after applying tells the candidate what happens next and cuts down on "did you get my CV?" calls. The job plugin can already send customizable notification emails, so this is mostly setup and good wording rather than new software. If it is already switched on, we will simply review the text.

From The DDC Group Balkans careersTo tarik.h@example.comSubject We received your application: German language, Customer Support Agent

Hi Tarik,

Thank you for applying. Your application and CV have reached our recruitment team in Sarajevo and Belgrade.

We read every application. If your profile matches the role, we will call you, usually within [your usual time frame]. The next step is normally a short interview and a language check.

The DDC Group Balkans recruitment team

Wording is a first draft. The time frame is yours to set; we will not put a promise in it that the team cannot keep.

How it is done

Everything happens inside the existing WordPress site and the WP Job Openings plugin. Nothing about the site's design changes.

  1. Check the plugin version. WP Job Openings (now called HireZoot) already has an automatic reply to applicants in its free version, and lets us add form fields with a small add-on. The paid Pro pack adds a form builder we can use instead. Either way the result is the same, and no paid licence is required.
  2. Add the form fields and the automatic email, test them on a copy of the site, then switch them on.
  3. Convert the current ads to text, keeping each poster as the image, and set up the template for new ads.
  4. Tidy up the typos, category names and image descriptions.

Included

  • Four new form fields, and setting up the automatic email
  • Text versions of the currently open ads, and a template for new ones
  • Correct job data for Google
  • Typo and category clean-up

Needs from you

  • Admin access to WordPress, or a contact at whoever manages it
  • Approval of the new questions and the email wording
  • Consent wording checked by your legal or HR team
  • Nothing to buy: the free version of the plugin is enough
