Smaller ideas

Each of these is useful on its own and small enough to add to any of the proposals, or to do separately. Pick any, all, or none.

Site tidy-up

Correct "norvegian language" to "Norwegian language", make every job category use the same capitalisation, fix "Colarado" and "Metro Manilla" in the footer, and give every ad image a proper description.

The footer may be shared with the other DDC Group sites. If so, we pass the correction to whoever manages it rather than change it ourselves.

Interview booking link

Instead of calling back and forth to find a time, you send one link and the candidate picks a free slot. It goes straight into your calendar, with a reminder to both of you.

If DDC uses Microsoft 365, Microsoft Bookings may already be included in your licence. Otherwise a tool such as Cal.com does the same. We set it up, connect it to your calendar and add the link to the message templates.

Low
Rough effort
1 to 3 days
Needs
Your calendar system

"Know someone?" referral page

A simple page that lists the languages you find hardest to hire, such as Norwegian, Czech or Hungarian, with a short form: "I know someone who speaks this". Your team shares it with friends. Referrals land in the candidate list marked with who referred them.

Whether there is a referral bonus, and how much, is DDC's decision. The page works either way.

"What happens after you apply" page

One clear page, linked from every ad and from the confirmation email: the steps (application, call, interview, language check, offer), roughly how long each takes, and what to prepare. Candidates know what to expect, and you get fewer "any news?" calls.

"Life at DDC" on the careers page

Today the careers page tells candidates about DDC in general terms. Real people would do more: short stories from agents in each language team, photos of the Sarajevo and Belgrade offices, and "a day in the role" for customer support and B2B sales. The same material can feed the existing Life at DDC Instagram.

This is F Koncept's core work as an employer branding agency. It needs a photo or video day on site and a few colleagues willing to take part.

It makes the offer clearer and more human. We do not promise a specific rise in applications from it.

Medium
Rough effort
