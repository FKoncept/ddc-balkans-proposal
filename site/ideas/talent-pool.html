How messages are sent

By default a message opens in your own email program, already filled in, or is copied for WhatsApp or Viber. You send it yourself, from your own address, and it is logged on the person's record.

Sending directly from the list, from a DDC address, is also possible. It needs a short setup by whoever manages your email domain, so we list it as an option rather than assume it.

Messages available in English at the start. More languages can be added whenever you write or approve the text.

A shortlist the moment a role opens

When a new role is published, the list looks through past applicants and shows who could fit. It only includes people who agreed to be kept on file, are not working here, and are not marked do-not-contact.

Housekeeping, once a month

Candidate data should not be kept forever. Once a month the list shows records that have passed the retention period you set, so you can delete them or ask the person whether they want to stay on file.

Past your retention periodSample data
14 records older than 12 months with no consent to stay on fileDelete
5 records whose 12-month consent ends this monthAsk to renew

The tool applies your policy; it does not set it. The retention period and the wording come from your legal or HR team.
