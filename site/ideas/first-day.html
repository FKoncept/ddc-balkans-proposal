Where "employed" and "working" get mixed up

In the Excel today, someone can be "employed" without anyone being sure they have actually started. The gap between the offer and the first day is where that happens: documents are chased by phone, start dates change by email, and nobody notices a missing person until the day itself.

This proposal closes that gap. A person only becomes "Working" when you confirm they arrived on day one.

What the new hire sees

One personal link, sent when they accept the offer. No account, no password to remember. It works on any phone.

On the page

  • Start date, time and place, plus who to ask for. If you change the date, the page updates and they get a message.
  • A checklist of documents to upload or bring. The list is yours: we build it from what DDC asks for today.
  • Short forms for the details you need for the contract, so nobody retypes them from a photo.
  • "I'll be there" confirmation, with an automatic reminder two days before the start.
  • For people working from home: equipment pickup or delivery, and first login steps, if that applies.

What you see

Every upcoming start date as a group, with who is ready and who needs a nudge. One click sends a reminder.

Day one

On the morning of the start, you tick who arrived. With proposal 1, those people move to "Working" on their record automatically, with the date. Anyone who did not arrive stays visible, so you can call them the same day instead of finding out weeks later in the Excel.

How we would run it

  1. Map what happens today after a yes: which documents, which details, who prepares the contract, how the first day works.
  2. Agree the checklist and messages with you and whoever handles contracts.
  3. Build the page and the board, and try them with the next group of starters.
  4. Connect to the candidate list so the offer and day one update the record on their own.

Included

  • Personal page for each new hire, and the board for you
  • Document uploads and short forms, with the checklist you define
  • Automatic reminders and a day-one check-in
  • Stricter storage for ID copies, deleted or handed to HR after the start

Not included, or needs a decision

  • Signing contracts electronically: contracts stay in your current process
  • Deciding which documents are legally required: that comes from DDC
  • Replacing an HR or payroll system
  • If a separate HR colleague handles this stage, they use the board; your statuses still update
