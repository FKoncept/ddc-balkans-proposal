    How it works today

    Three steps, and two of them are done by hand.

    1. Someone applieson the careers website
    2. You copy it into Excelby hand, one by one
    3. You keep each status up to dateby hand, from memory

    Everything lives in one big file that nothing checks, so it slowly stops matching reality. For example:

    • Someone who already works here is still marked "to contact".
    • Someone who left gets a call about a new job.
    • The same person is in the file twice, because they applied for two jobs.

    This is based on what we were told about your process. If yours is different, that is exactly what we want to hear.

    The idea

    One list that fills itself and checks itself.

    Every application lands in one shared list on its own. Each person exists once, with their whole history, and the list warns you before you contact the wrong person.

    Around that list we suggest five changes. Each one fixes one problem. You can take one, some, or all of them.

    Pick one to jump straight to it, or press Next to go through them in order.

    Change 1 of 5: One candidate list

    No more copying, and no more duplicates.

    I copy every application into Excel, and the file keeps going out of date. Sometimes the same person is in there twice, with two different statuses.

    What changes
    Applications appear in one shared list on their own. When someone applies again, it is added to their existing record. People who work here, or asked not to be contacted, never appear in your call list.
    What it takes
    Bigger job about 5 to 8 weeks
    What we need
    Permission to add a small add-on to your careers website
    How does it work behind the scenes?

    A small add-on on the careers website (WordPress) sends each new application, CV included, to the list the moment it arrives. Candidates see no change.

    Your existing Excel is imported once. Possible duplicates are shown side by side, and you decide which are the same person. Nothing is merged on our guess.

    The list works in any browser, stores data in the EU, and you can download everything to Excel at any time.

    Change 2 of 5: Better applications

    Know someone's language, level and city without opening their CV.

    I have to open every CV to find out which language someone speaks, how well, and which city they want.

    What changes
    The form asks four more short questions: languages and level, preferred city, start date, and whether they want to stay on file for other jobs. Every applicant also gets a short "we received your application" email.
    What it takes
    Small job about 1 to 2 weeks
    What we need
    Access to your careers website
    What we noticed on your careers website

    Each job ad is one poster image. Phones, translation tools and Google cannot read the text inside it, including "German, minimum B2". We would add the same text under the poster, which stays.

    The job filter says "norvegian language", and the footer has "Colarado" and "Metro Manilla". We would fix those too.

    How does it work behind the scenes?

    Your job plugin (WP Job Openings) already supports extra form fields and an automatic reply in its free version. Nothing to buy; we set it up and write the wording with you.

    Change 3 of 5: Talent pool and follow-ups

    Nobody forgotten, and past applicants found again.

    I lose track of who I still need to call back. And when a new role opens, I start from zero, even though good people applied before.

    What changes
    A short list every morning: who is waiting, who did not answer, who has an interview. Ready-made messages, filled in with their name. When a new role opens, the list shows past applicants who fit and agreed to stay on file.
    What it takes
    Medium job about 3 to 4 weeks
    What we need
    Change 1 first, because this builds on the list
    How does it work behind the scenes?

    Messages open in your own email, already filled in, or are copied for WhatsApp or Viber. Sending straight from a DDC address is possible too, with a short setup by whoever manages your email.

    Once a month the list also shows records past the retention period you set, and a short summary of applications and hires per language.

    Change 4 of 5: Language check

    Language levels checked by your own colleagues, without arranging a call.

    I can't judge Norwegian or Czech myself, so for every candidate I have to find a colleague and a time that suits everyone.

    You probably already have a test. If so, tell us how it works: we would build around it rather than replace it.
    What changes
    The candidate records two or three short spoken answers and writes one short reply, on their phone, in about ten minutes. A colleague who speaks the language listens when it suits them and picks a level. The result appears on the candidate's record.
    What it takes
    Medium job about 3 to 4 weeks
    What we need
    Colleagues who speak each language, to adapt the questions and do the reviews
    What it is not

    No software decides the level: a person always does. It is an internal check, not a certificate. Reviewers see a candidate code instead of a name, and recordings are deleted after the decision.

    Change 5 of 5: From yes to first day

    Documents, start dates and day one, all in one place.

    After someone says yes, I chase documents and start dates by phone. Sometimes a person is marked "employed" and never actually starts.

    What changes
    Each new hire gets one personal page: start date, what to bring, documents to upload, and an "I'll be there" button. You see who is ready. On day one you tick who arrived, and they become "Working".
    What it takes
    Medium job about 3 to 4 weeks
    What we need
    Your list of documents and first-day details. Works best with change 1.
    What it is not

    Contracts are not signed online; they stay in your current process. Which documents are legally required is DDC's decision. ID copies are stored more strictly and deleted or handed to HR after the start.

    Straight answers

    What we promise, and what we don't.

    We will

    • Build only what you choose, adjusted to how you really work.
    • Keep your careers website; candidates apply exactly as before.
    • Never merge two records without your say.
    • Keep candidate data in the EU, open only to the people you name, under a written data agreement.
    • Show you how it works and leave short written instructions.

    We won't claim

    • That you will get more applicants or rank higher on Google.
    • That the list stays right if nobody updates it. It warns and reminds; a person still records what happened.
    • Legal compliance. We build tools for your rules; the rules come from your legal or HR team.
    • That software can judge candidates. People decide, always.
