    How it works today

    Two steps run on their own. Three depend on one person's memory.

    1. A candidate appliesOn the careers site, through the WP Job Openings form.
    2. WordPress stores itName, email, phone, bio and CV land in the site's admin.
    3. Copied into ExcelEach application is retyped or pasted into the big sheet.
    4. Statuses updatedContacted, employed, working, not working: all edited by hand.
    5. The sheet driftsNothing checks it, so it slowly stops matching reality.
    Contacted, but already workingAn older row still says "to contact" for someone who started last month.
    Left, then contacted againSomeone quits, nobody updates the row, and they get a call about a new role.
    One person, several rowsApplying for German support and then for B2B sales creates two separate people.

    What we noticed on the careers site

    The site works. A few details make the recruiter's job harder.

    We only looked at the public pages of balkans.theddcgroup.com. Nothing here needs a new website: each item is a small, contained fix.

    Job ads are pictures

    Each ad is a single poster image with no text version. Google, screen readers and translation tools cannot read "German, minimum B2", duties or benefits.

    The form asks for five things

    Name, email, phone, bio and CV. It does not ask which languages and at what level, which city, or when someone can start, so the list cannot be filtered by any of those.

    norvegian languageColaradoMetro Manilla

    Small typos in public places

    The job filter lists "norvegian language", and the footer says "Colarado" and "Metro Manilla". Minutes to fix, but candidates notice.

    Proposal 1

    One candidate list

    Every application arrives on its own. Every person exists once. Every status change shows who made it and when.

    • No more copying. New applications appear in the list within minutes of being sent.
    • One record per person. A second application from the same email or phone is added to the existing record, not a new row.
    • Guardrails. People marked working or do-not-contact never show up in "to contact" lists.
    • Your Excel comes with you. We import it, and you confirm the likely duplicates we find.

    Proposal 2

    Better applications

    Ask for what you filter by, write ads that can be read, and answer every applicant straight away.

    • Four new form fields. Languages with level, preferred city, earliest start date, and consent to be kept on file for future roles.
    • Job ads as real text. Your poster stays as the picture; the requirements, duties and benefits become readable text underneath.
    • Correct job data for Google. The job details the site already sends to Google get the real ad text instead of an image tag.
    • Automatic confirmation email telling the candidate what happens next.
    Low Rough effort 1 to 2 weeks

    Proposal 3

    Talent pool and follow-ups

    The list tells you who needs a call today, and who to call first when a new language opens up.

    • Reminders. "Contacted 7 days ago, no answer yet" appears on its own, so nobody is forgotten.

    Smaller ideas

    Things worth doing alongside, each small on its own.

    Site tidy-up

    Low

    Fix the typos, make the job category names consistent, add alt text to the ad images.

    Interview booking link

    Low

    Candidates pick a free slot themselves, using Microsoft Bookings or a similar tool you may already have.

    "Know someone?" referral page

    Low

    A simple page your team can share when a hard language like Norwegian or Czech is open.

    Chat buttons on each record

    Low

    One click opens WhatsApp or Viber with the number filled in. Part of proposal 1 at no extra effort.

    "What happens after you apply" page

    Low

    The steps, rough timings and what to prepare, linked from every ad. Fewer "any news?" calls.

    "Life at DDC" careers content

    Medium

    Real people, the office and a day in each role on the careers page. This is F Koncept's core work.

    Straight answers

    What we promise, and what we don't.

    We will

    • Build what you choose from these mockups, adjusted to your real columns and statuses.
    • Keep the careers site and the WP Job Openings plugin; candidates apply exactly as before.
    • Import your existing Excel and show you likely duplicates to confirm, not merge them blindly.
    • With the recommended build, host candidate data in the EU and give access only to the people you name.
    • Train you and hand over short written instructions.

    We won't claim

    • That you will get more applicants or rank higher on Google. Readable ads help; they guarantee nothing.
    • That the list is right if nobody updates it. It warns and reminds, but a person still records the outcome of a call.
    • Legal compliance. We build the tools for your retention rules; the rules and wording come from your legal or HR team.
    • Automatic candidate scoring. We don't recommend letting software rank people.
    • A link to your HR or payroll system, unless it can export a list we can read.
